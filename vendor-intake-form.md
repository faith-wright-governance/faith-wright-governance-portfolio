# Vendor Intake Form

## Purpose

The Vendor Intake Form is used to collect initial information about a supplier before due diligence activities begin.

* Personal Data? (Yes/No)
* Financial Data? (Yes/No)

* Approved for Due Diligence
* Additional Information Required

Creating a Vendor Intake Form helped me understand how organisations collect supplier information before beginning vendor due diligence, risk assessment, and onboarding activities.

# AI Vendor Risk Assessment

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) assessment for an AI software vendor.

## Vendor Information

Vendor Name: Example AI Solutions

Service Provided:
AI-powered document analysis and workflow automation.

## Assessment Scope

- Information Security
- Data Protection
- Business Continuity
- AI Governance
- Regulatory Compliance

## Risk Assessment

### Information Security Risk

Risk Level: Medium

Observations:
- Encryption available
- Multi-factor authentication supported
- Annual security reviews conducted

### Data Protection Risk

Risk Level: Medium

Observations:
- Processes personal data
- GDPR compliance statement available
- Data retention controls documented

### AI Governance Risk

Risk Level: High

Observations:
- Limited explainability
- Model training methodology not fully disclosed
- Human oversight required

### Operational Risk

Risk Level: Low

Observations:
- Strong uptime history
- Business continuity plan documented

## Recommended Controls

- Vendor due diligence review
- Annual reassessment
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- AI governance review
- Contractual security requirements

## Final Risk Rating

Medium Risk

## Recommendation

Approve vendor subject to implementation of recommended controls and ongoing monitoring.

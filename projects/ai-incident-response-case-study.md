# AI Incident Response Case Study

## Project Overview

This case study demonstrates how an organisation should respond to an AI-related security and governance incident.

The objective is to identify risks, contain the incident, assess impact, and implement corrective actions.

---

## Incident Summary

An employee uploaded confidential customer information into a public AI chatbot without approval.

The information included:

- Customer names
- Email addresses
- Internal business data

The AI platform was not approved for organisational use.

---

## Incident Classification

| Category | Classification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Incident Type | Data Breach |
| Severity | High |
| Risk Level | High |
| Regulatory Impact | Potential GDPR Breach |

---

## Immediate Response Actions

### Containment

- Stop further use of the AI platform
- Notify Information Governance team
- Notify Security team
- Preserve evidence

### Investigation

- Identify affected data
- Identify affected individuals
- Determine root cause
- Review access logs

---

## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Rating |
|--------|--------|------------|---------|
| Data Disclosure | High | Medium | High |
| Regulatory Fine | High | Low | Medium |
| Reputational Damage | High | Medium | High |
| Loss of Trust | High | Medium | High |

---

## Root Cause Analysis

### Findings

1. Lack of AI governance policy
2. Insufficient employee awareness
3. No approved AI usage guidelines
4. Lack of monitoring controls

---

## Corrective Actions

### Short-Term

- Disable unauthorised AI access
- Conduct staff awareness training
- Notify Data Protection Officer

### Long-Term

- Implement AI Governance Framework
- Create AI Acceptable Use Policy
- Establish AI Risk Register
- Perform regular audits

---

## Compliance Considerations

- UK GDPR
- Data Protection Act 2018
- NHS DSP Toolkit
- ISO 27001
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework

---

## Outcome

The incident was successfully contained and corrective actions were implemented to reduce future risk.

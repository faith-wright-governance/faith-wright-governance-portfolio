# AI Governance Framework

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an AI Governance Framework designed to support the safe, ethical and compliant use of Artificial Intelligence within an organisation.

## Purpose

The framework helps organisations:

- Manage AI risks
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Protect personal data
- Maintain accountability
- Support ethical AI use

## Governance Structure

### Board Oversight

Responsible for:

- Strategic direction
- Risk oversight
- Governance approval

### Risk and Compliance Team

Responsible for:

- Risk assessments
- Compliance monitoring
- Policy development

### Technical Teams

Responsible for:

- AI model development
- Security controls
- Testing and validation

## Key Risk Areas

### Data Privacy

Risks:

- Personal data misuse
- Unauthorised access

Controls:

- Access controls
- Data minimisation
- Encryption

### Bias and Fairness

Risks:

- Discrimination
- Unfair outcomes

Controls:

- Bias testing
- Human review

### Transparency

Risks:

- Lack of explainability

Controls:

- Documentation
- Audit trails

### Security

Risks:

- Model manipulation
- Data breaches

Controls:

- Security testing
- Monitoring

## AI Lifecycle Governance

1. Design
2. Development
3. Testing
4. Deployment
5. Monitoring
6. Retirement

## Compliance Considerations

- UK GDPR
- Data Protection Act 2018
- NHS DSP Toolkit
- ISO 27001
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework

## Outcome

The framework provides a structured approach to managing AI risks while enabling innovation.

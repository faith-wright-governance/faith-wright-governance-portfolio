# Practice Risk Assessment

## Scenario

A cloud software vendor will store customer personal data.

## Risk Identified

Unauthorised access to customer information.

## Impact

High

## Likelihood

Medium

## Risk Rating

High

## Mitigation

- Multi-factor authentication
- Encryption
- Vendor security review
- Annual reassessment

## Residual Risk

Medium

## Learning Reflection

This exercise helped me understand how risks are identified, assessed and treated within a governance and compliance environment.

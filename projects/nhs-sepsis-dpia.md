# NHS Sepsis AI DPIA Example

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) could be conducted for an AI system used to support early sepsis detection within an NHS hospital environment.

## Purpose

The objective is to assess privacy, security, governance, and compliance risks associated with processing patient information through an AI-assisted clinical decision support tool.

## Data Processed

- Patient identifiers
- Clinical observations
- Vital signs
- Laboratory results
- Medical history

## Potential Risks

### Privacy Risks

- Unauthorised access to patient data
- Excessive data collection
- Inappropriate data sharing

### AI Risks

- False positive alerts
- False negative alerts
- Lack of explainability
- Over-reliance on AI outputs

### Governance Risks

- Inadequate oversight
- Lack of accountability
- Incomplete audit trails

## Mitigation Controls

- Role-based access control
- Encryption at rest and in transit
- Clinical review before action
- Regular AI performance monitoring
- Staff training
- DPIA review and approval process

## Compliance Considerations

- UK GDPR
- Data Protection Act 2018
- NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit
- Information Governance requirements

## Outcome

The proposed AI solution can be implemented provided appropriate governance controls, monitoring arrangements, and data protection safeguards are maintained.

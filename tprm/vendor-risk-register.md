# Vendor Risk Register

## Purpose

A Vendor Risk Register is a central repository used to track third-party suppliers, their risk ratings, review dates, due diligence status, and oversight activities.

It helps organisations maintain visibility of vendor risks and supports Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) processes.

## Risk Tiers

### Critical

Vendor failure would significantly impact business operations or regulatory obligations.

### High

Vendor has access to sensitive data or supports critical business services.

### Medium

Vendor supports important business functions but has limited access to sensitive information.

### Low

Vendor has minimal business impact and limited access to information.

## Key Fields

* Vendor Name
* Legal Entity
* Service Description
* Business Owner
* Risk Tier
* Data Classification
* Contract Start Date
* Contract Expiry Date
* Last Assessment Date
* Next Review Date
* Outstanding Findings
* Current Status

## Benefits

A Vendor Risk Register provides:

* Centralised vendor oversight
* Consistent risk classification
* Improved regulatory compliance
* Better audit evidence
* Enhanced third-party governance
* Improved risk visibility

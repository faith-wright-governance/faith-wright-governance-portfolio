# TPRM Agentic AI Use Case Register

## Use Case 01: AI-Powered Vendor Due Diligence Agent

## Purpose

This use case explains how an AI agent can support the Third-Party Risk Management lifecycle by automating vendor due diligence activities.

## Description

The AI agent collects and analyses vendor information from questionnaires, certificates, public records, sanctions databases, cyber risk sources, financial health reports and procurement systems.

It then generates a vendor risk score, assigns a risk tier, identifies red flags, and produces a due diligence summary report for human review.

## Business Rationale

Manual vendor due diligence can be slow, inconsistent and time-consuming. An AI agent can help reduce onboarding delays, improve consistency, and support risk teams by automating data gathering, document review and first-level risk analysis.

## Trigger Conditions

* New vendor onboarding request
* Existing vendor renewal
* Vendor service scope change
* Annual vendor reassessment
* Regulatory or policy change

## Data Inputs

* Vendor due diligence questionnaire
* ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certificates
* Financial health reports
* Sanctions and PEP screening results
* Cyber risk rating data
* Procurement system records

## Data Outputs

* Vendor risk score
* Risk tier classification
* Due diligence summary report
* Risk findings
* Recommended actions
* Escalation alert for high-risk vendors

## Human-in-the-Loop Requirement

A human analyst must review the final risk score and approve the vendor risk tier before onboarding is completed.

Critical or high-risk vendors should be escalated to a senior risk owner.

## Risk and Compliance Considerations

* GDPR
* Vendor confidentiality
* Audit trail
* Explainability
* Human approval
* Risk acceptance governance
* Model performance monitoring

## AI Risk Classification

Medium

The AI agent supports decision-making but should not fully approve or reject vendors without human oversight.

## Business Owner

Head of Third-Party Risk Management

## Technical Owner

AI / Automation Team

## Approver

Chief Risk Officer or Senior Risk Owner

## Value

This use case can reduce manual due diligence effort, improve consistency, speed up vendor onboarding, and create stronger audit evidence for third-party risk decisions.

## Career Reflection

This use case helped me understand how AI agents can support TPRM by automating data collection, risk scoring, due diligence review and reporting while keeping humans responsible for final decisions.

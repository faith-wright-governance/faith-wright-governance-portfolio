# Vendor Due Diligence Process

## Purpose

Vendor Due Diligence is the process of assessing suppliers before onboarding or contract renewal to identify risks.

## Key Steps

### 1. Vendor Intake
Collect supplier information.

### 2. Due Diligence Questionnaire (DDQ)
Gather governance, compliance and security information.

### 3. External Checks
- Companies House
- Financial checks
- Sanctions screening
- PEP screening

### 4. Risk Assessment
Assess:
- Operational risk
- Cybersecurity risk
- Compliance risk
- Financial risk

### 5. Risk Classification
Assign:
- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

### 6. Ongoing Monitoring
Review vendors regularly and update risk ratings.

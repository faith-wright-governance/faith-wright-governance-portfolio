## Key Takeaway

Vendor due diligence is a structured process used to identify, assess and manage risks associated with suppliers before engaging with them.

## Concepts Learned

### Vendor Intake Form

Used to collect vendor information such as company details, contacts, services and jurisdictions.

### Vendor Tracker

A central repository used to monitor vendor status, risk ratings, reviews and due diligence progress.

### DDQ (Due Diligence Questionnaire)

A structured questionnaire used to assess security, compliance, governance, operational resilience and vendor risk.

### Companies House API

Used to verify company registration information and corporate details.

### OpenSanctions API

Used to identify sanctions exposure and politically exposed persons (PEPs).

### AI Vendor Due Diligence Agent

An automated workflow that collects information from multiple sources, analyses risk and generates due diligence reports.

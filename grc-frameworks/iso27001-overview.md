# ISO 27001 Overview

## Purpose

ISO 27001 is the international standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS).

It provides a framework for managing information security risks and protecting confidential, sensitive, and business-critical information.

## Why ISO 27001 Matters

Organisations use ISO 27001 to:

* Protect information assets
* Reduce cybersecurity risks
* Demonstrate security compliance
* Improve customer trust
* Meet regulatory requirements

## What is an ISMS?

An Information Security Management System (ISMS) is a structured approach to managing:

* People
* Processes
* Technology

to protect information assets.

## Key Principles

### Confidentiality

Ensure information is only accessible to authorised individuals.

### Integrity

Ensure information remains accurate and complete.

### Availability

Ensure information is available when needed.

## Risk-Based Approach

ISO 27001 requires organisations to:

1. Identify risks
2. Assess risks
3. Treat risks
4. Monitor risks
5. Continuously improve controls

## Common Security Controls

Examples include:

* Access control
* Password management
* Security awareness training
* Incident management
* Supplier security management
* Asset management
* Backup and recovery
* Business continuity planning

## Relationship to TPRM

ISO 27001 supports Third-Party Risk Management by requiring organisations to:

* Assess supplier security risks
* Monitor third-party providers
* Define security requirements in contracts
* Manage supplier access to information

## Benefits

* Improved information security
* Better risk management
* Regulatory compliance
* Increased customer confidence
* Stronger governance

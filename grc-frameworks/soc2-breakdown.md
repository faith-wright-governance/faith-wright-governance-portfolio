# SOC 2 Overview

## Purpose

SOC 2 is a security and compliance framework developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA).

It helps organisations demonstrate that they manage customer data securely.

## Trust Service Criteria

### Security

Protect systems against unauthorised access.

### Availability

Ensure systems remain available for use.

### Processing Integrity

Ensure data is processed accurately and completely.

### Confidentiality

Protect sensitive information.

### Privacy

Protect personal information.

## Relationship to TPRM

SOC 2 reports are commonly reviewed during vendor due diligence to assess the effectiveness of supplier security controls.

## Learning Reflection

Studying SOC 2 helped me understand how organisations demonstrate security assurance and compliance through independent audits.

# NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) Notes

## Purpose

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) is a risk-based framework used by organisations to identify, assess, manage, and reduce cybersecurity risks.

It provides a common language for understanding and improving cybersecurity posture.

## Why NIST CSF Matters

The framework helps organisations:

* Understand cybersecurity risks
* Protect critical systems and information
* Detect security incidents
* Respond effectively to cyber events
* Recover from disruptions

## Core Functions

### 1. Govern

Establish cybersecurity governance, policies, oversight, risk management strategy, and accountability.

### 2. Identify

Understand organisational assets, systems, data, suppliers, business environment, and risks.

Examples:

* Asset inventories
* Risk assessments
* Business impact analysis
* Vendor identification

### 3. Protect

Implement safeguards to reduce cybersecurity risk.

Examples:

* Access controls
* Security awareness training
* Data protection measures
* Multi-factor authentication

### 4. Detect

Identify cybersecurity events quickly.

Examples:

* Security monitoring
* Threat detection
* Log analysis
* Continuous monitoring

### 5. Respond

Take action when an incident occurs.

Examples:

* Incident response plans
* Communications procedures
* Containment activities
* Investigation processes

### 6. Recover

Restore normal operations after an incident.

Examples:

* Business continuity
* Disaster recovery
* Lessons learned reviews
* System restoration

## Relationship to TPRM

NIST CSF supports Third-Party Risk Management by helping organisations:

* Identify vendors
* Assess supplier risks
* Monitor third-party cybersecurity controls
* Respond to vendor-related incidents

## Learning Reflection

Studying the NIST Cybersecurity Framework helped me understand how organisations manage cybersecurity risks through governance, protection, detection, response, and recovery activities while supporting regulatory compliance and risk management objectives.
